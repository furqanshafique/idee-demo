## 1. Business purpose and scope
IDEE (Innovation Discovery Engine) helps industrial SMEs find new sellable products, components or services by matching actual capabilities with external market and technology signals. It does not merely generate ideas or list patents. The workflow is: reference project -> work performed -> transferable capability -> possible application -> real source -> hypothesis with limitations. Two directions: technology push (what can we do elsewhere?) and market pull (what problem exists, and can we contribute?).

Ergo-Tec publicly describes development and production of patient positioning systems and industrial special-purpose machinery. It already offers test systems; do not claim testing is a new industry for it. Our fictional reference project UL-01 is consistent with the earlier Digital Product Dossier demo. No actual confidential data or measured Ergo-Tec capacity has been supplied. Use the exact demo data and real-public-source distinction in `data/seed.json` and `data/public_sources.json`.

The full Michael Reble specification is in `references/`. It describes eight steps, a 628-field catalog, two patent stages, multi-country search, scoring and a management decision. Do NOT build all of that this week. A working evidence-linked intake and search are mandatory; advanced matching and opportunity card are limited previews. The production-quality investment recommendation is explicitly out of scope for Friday.
